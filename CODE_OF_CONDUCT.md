# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ORBIT AI to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity or preferred model vendor. We pledge to make participation harassment-free
for all contributors.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good faith; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy: never publish another person's credentials, logs or personal data.
- Credit others' work and follow the project's licensing terms.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption of discussions, and publishing private
information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and when
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behaviour violates this code, and will explain decisions where appropriate.
